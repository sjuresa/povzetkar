# Project Lifecycle Overview

**Version:** 1.1
**Date:** November 1, 2025

## 1. Introduction

This document outlines the high-level lifecycle of the Povzetkar project. It describes the strategic phases that govern the project's long-term direction, complementing the more tactical, version-focused `11_development_checklist.md`.

---

## Phase 1: Strategic Planning & Vision

*This phase defines the long-term goals and direction of the project.*

- **Activity:** Define and maintain the project's vision and high-level goals.
- **Artifact:** `docs/overview/roadmap.md`
- **Frequency:** As needed, but reviewed during each Strategic Review phase.

---

## Phase 2: Development Execution Cycle

*This phase covers the development of a single, concrete version of the software.*

- **Activity:** Execute the end-to-end process of planning, developing, testing, and releasing a software version.
- **Artifact:** The process is detailed in `docs/sdlc/11_development_checklist.md`.

---

## Phase 3: Periodic Strategic Review
*This phase ensures the project remains aligned with its strategic goals and that the development process is effective.*
- **Activity:** Review the project's long-term roadmap, the effectiveness of the development methodology, and the overall health of the project documentation.
- **Artifacts:**
    - Review and update `docs/overview/roadmap.md`.
    - Review and potentially update `docs/sdlc/07_development_methodology_comparison.md` and `AGENT.md`.
    - Assess key documentation (`DEVELOPMENT_ANALYSIS.md`, `06_development_process.md`, etc.) for staleness to ensure the post-version update process is effective.
- **Frequency:** Periodically (e.g., quarterly, after major milestones).
