**Date:** *To be filled upon completion*

## 1. Overview

Version 0.6 was a major milestone focused on migrating the entire core workflow from the legacy application into the new Quarkus-based system. The goal was to achieve a fully autonomous application capable of fetching content, summarizing it, and generating briefings without manual intervention.

This version also saw a significant strategic shift away from a temporary Git-based publishing mechanism towards implementing a foundational, server-side rendered user interface.
